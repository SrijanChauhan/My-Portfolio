Financial Software and Systems · Grameen Pay · 2020–2023 · Financial Inclusion

Taking banking to towns the banks don't reach

Financial Inclusion Agent Banking 0→1

India's unbanked population needed a channel, not another app nobody could use. Grameen Pay is an agent-assisted platform bundling loans, insurance, AEPS and DMT for underserved towns — built to work through a trusted local agent rather than asking someone with no banking history to trust an app.

To ground the GTM strategy in how rural consumers actually behave rather than in assumptions, I ran first-hand interviews with 1,154 people and 7 focus group discussions across 4 regions (Bhopal, Gwalior, Indore, Jabalpur) and 28 districts — in choupals, markets and deep into the villages. Respondents spanned 338 shop owners, 325 farmers, 223 daily-wage earners and 106 salaried workers.

That research shaped a financial-inclusion strategy that piloted within 3 months, then rolled out nationally with 22 developers and 10+ executives.

Four customer personas

The segments the research surfaced, used to shape messaging and the agent-training script.

Traditionalists

Price-led

Conservative rural consumers who rely on conventional channels to make purchase decisions. Necessity drives most decisions, and they typically buy the cheapest offering available.

Steady Climbers

Aspirational

They aspire to a more comfortable lifestyle and want to enhance their social standing among peers — and strive to do so by buying branded products and services.

Young Enthusiasts

Digital-native

Rural consumers aged 18–28 who make extensive use of digital technology and buy branded products to enhance their social image. They powerfully influence other segments' decisions.

Village Elites

Quality-led

Progressive rural consumers with high education and awareness levels. They expect the best in product quality, features and service.

The purchase journey

Six stages, designed around a local agent rather than a self-serve app.

01 · AWARENESS

Local, in-language, relatable

Customers get a deep understanding of the product through events organized locally by the on-ground team, in vernacular language, with simple, customised messaging.

02 · CONSIDERATION

Products built for this market

A tailored value proposition for the rural market — products customised specifically for the target customer — to make the purchase decision easier.

03 · VALIDATION

Trusted local voices

Village heads act as trusted advisors; BC agents are trained to serve as informed advisors who validate the purchase.

04 · PURCHASE

Agent-assisted, chatbot-backed

Customers buy through the terminal with the BC agent's help, with CRM and chatbot assistance for any friction in the process.

05 · EXPERIENCE

Support at the point of need

BC agents assist with claims or deposit challenges; ease of use, accessibility, and a customer success desk gather insights to keep improving.

06 · ADVOCACY

Village heads as advocates

Village heads and BC agents are nurtured as advocates, leveraging word of mouth and delivering on the brand promise to win loyalty.

Outcome

3 mo from 1,154 prospect interviews to a live pilot — then a national rollout across 22 developers and 10+ executives.
